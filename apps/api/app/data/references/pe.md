# Local reference: suspected pulmonary embolism (pe)

> Illustrative demo scaffolding for the triage workstation. Not a validated
> clinical protocol. The nurse and treating clinician remain the decision-makers.

§1 In patients with pleuritic chest pain and thromboembolic risk factors
(recent immobilization, prior VTE, estrogen therapy), pulmonary embolism
should remain in the differential at triage.

§2 A 12-lead ECG (ECG-12) is reasonable in pleuritic chest pain to assess
right-heart strain patterns and to evaluate alternative cardiac causes.

§3 D-dimer testing (LAB-DDIMER) can help stratify low-probability patients;
it is not part of the initial triage lab panel at this site.

§4 CT pulmonary angiography (IMG-CTPA) is the definitive imaging study when
pre-test probability is moderate or high; imaging orders are made by the
treating clinician, not at triage.

§5 A complete blood count (LAB-CBC) and metabolic panel (LAB-CMP) establish a
baseline before anticoagulation decisions are made downstream.
