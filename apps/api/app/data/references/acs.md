# Local reference: suspected acute coronary syndrome (acs)

> Illustrative demo scaffolding for the triage workstation. Not a validated
> clinical protocol. The nurse and treating clinician remain the decision-makers.

§1 In adults with pressure-like chest pain, radiation to arm or jaw, or
autonomic features, an early 12-lead ECG (ECG-12) supports timely triage and
should be obtained promptly.

§2 High-sensitivity troponin I (LAB-TROP-HS) is the preferred initial
biomarker when a cardiac cause of chest pain is being considered.

§3 A complete blood count (LAB-CBC) helps identify anemia as a contributor to
demand ischemia in patients with exertional chest discomfort.

§4 A comprehensive metabolic panel (LAB-CMP) provides renal function and
electrolytes relevant to interpretation of cardiac workup and later imaging
decisions.

§5 Where symptoms and initial results are equivocal, departments may consider
a chest X-ray (IMG-CXR) to assess alternative thoracic causes; this is an
imaging decision outside the initial triage panel.
