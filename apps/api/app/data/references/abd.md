# Local reference: abdominal pain (abd)

> Illustrative demo scaffolding for the triage workstation. Not a validated
> clinical protocol. The nurse and treating clinician remain the decision-makers.

§1 Location, migration, and meal relationship narrow the abdominal pain
differential at triage; fever, GI bleeding, or obstipation escalate urgency.

§2 A complete blood count (LAB-CBC) screens for infection and blood loss in
undifferentiated abdominal pain.

§3 A comprehensive metabolic panel (LAB-CMP) assesses electrolytes, renal
function, and liver enzymes relevant to biliary and obstructive patterns.

§4 Lipase testing (LAB-LIPASE) is the preferred test for suspected
pancreatitis; it is not part of the initial triage panel at this site.

§5 Abdominal CT (IMG-CT-ABD) and ultrasound (IMG-US-ABD) are imaging
decisions for the treating clinician and are not orderable from triage.
