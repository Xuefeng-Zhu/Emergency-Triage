# Local reference: undifferentiated chest pain (chest-pain-general)

> Illustrative demo scaffolding for the triage workstation. Not a validated
> clinical protocol. The nurse and treating clinician remain the decision-makers.

§1 Undifferentiated chest pain at triage is characterized by onset, character,
exertional relationship, and radiation before a working pattern is assigned.

§2 An early 12-lead ECG (ECG-12) is appropriate for most adult chest pain
presentations at triage.

§3 High-sensitivity troponin I (LAB-TROP-HS) is the biomarker of choice when
any cardiac concern exists.

§4 A complete blood count (LAB-CBC) and comprehensive metabolic panel
(LAB-CMP) establish a baseline for downstream decisions.
