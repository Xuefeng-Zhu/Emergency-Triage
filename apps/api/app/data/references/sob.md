# Local reference: shortness of breath (sob)

> Illustrative demo scaffolding for the triage workstation. Not a validated
> clinical protocol. The nurse and treating clinician remain the decision-makers.

§1 Sudden-onset breathlessness raises concern for pulmonary embolism or
pneumothorax; gradual onset with orthopnea suggests a cardiac contribution.

§2 A 12-lead ECG (ECG-12) helps evaluate cardiac contributions to
breathlessness at triage.

§3 A complete blood count (LAB-CBC) screens for anemia and infection as
contributors to breathlessness.

§4 A comprehensive metabolic panel (LAB-CMP) provides renal function and
electrolytes relevant to fluid and diuretic decisions made downstream.

§5 A chest X-ray (IMG-CXR) is the usual first imaging study for undifferentiated
breathlessness; imaging is ordered by the treating clinician, not at triage.
